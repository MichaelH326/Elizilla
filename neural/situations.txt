# Situation map for the neural Elizilla's training data and eval.
#
# One situation per line:   id | what happened (first person) | what it's about | mood
#   - "what happened" is how a person might first say it
#   - "what it's about" is the thing itself, as the person would name it
#   - mood is one of: anxious sad angry happy tired lonely bored proud guilty
#     confused excited nervous frustrated calm mixed
# Areas start with "## ". About 15% of situations are held out of training
# (picked by situations.py from the id, so the split never changes).
# Nothing here is about suicide, self-harm, abuse or violence: that is
# safety.py's job, never the model's.
